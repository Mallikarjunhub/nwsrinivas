How the fellowship runs

Built for one candidate, reviewed like a production team

Srinivas of Whitefield, Bengaluru, is the sole mentee of this cohort. Every session is live, every week ends in a committed artefact, and nothing is marked complete on trust alone.

Directing mentor

Mallikarjun S Nandyal — Director of Nammaweb AI Research Labs and Principal Systems Architect. He sets the weekly brief, reviews the code line by line on Fridays and chairs the milestone defences.

Two phases, one gate

Phase 1 builds the theory and the sovereign-edge stack. Phase 2 is a production workshop and placement push. Entry to Phase 2 requires 80% in the week 12 technical defence.

Proof over attendance

Progress is measured in repositories, latency numbers and recall scores. The certificate is issued against submitted artefacts, not hours logged.

Sovereign by default

Half the programme runs on hardware you own — quantized models, local vector stores, air-gapped retrieval. Useful when the data cannot leave the building.

Bengaluru placement work

The final month is résumé rewriting in metrics, published case studies, a 40-company target list and fifty timed technical drills.

What you need before week 1

Working knowledge of JavaScript and the MERN stack, comfort with Git, and a machine with 16 GB RAM. No prior Python or machine-learning background is assumed.
